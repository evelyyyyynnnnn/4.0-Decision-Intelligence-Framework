Why the split is chronological

Real markets give one history

real markets give one history, so the only honest split is in time. A random split leaks the test period's regime into the training set and restores the stationarity guarantee that makes the simulated comparison easy.

The cost of being honest about it is visible in the table: the worst training day is 11.5% against 6.2% in the test window. Those are the real returns that fell in those windows.

The staffing scenario stays simulated

there is no public series of per-unit hospital staffing demand to download; a proxy would not make it a staffing study.
